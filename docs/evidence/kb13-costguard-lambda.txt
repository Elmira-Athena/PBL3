Timezone la Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh, KHONG phai UTC. Neu de UTC thi cron(0 0) se
chay luc 7 gio SANG gio Viet Nam — tuc tat ha tang giua ngay lam viec.
LUU Y khi doc log CloudWatch: log ghi theo UTC, nen 00:00 ICT hien ra la
17:00 UTC cua NGAY HOM TRUOC.

reserved_concurrency = null, KHONG phai 1 nhu plan viet. Da do thuc te:
  aws lambda get-account-settings -> ConcurrentExecutions: 10
AWS luon giu toi thieu 10 unreserved concurrency, nen dat reserved = 1 lam
apply chet voi InvalidParameterValueException. Bu o tang ma: doc trang thai
truoc khi ghi, va bat rieng InvalidDBInstanceState.
