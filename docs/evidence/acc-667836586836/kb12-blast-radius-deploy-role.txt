### KB-12: ban kinh anh huong (blast radius) cua role deploy cua GitHub Actions
Ngay do: 2026-08-23. Account 667836586836, region ap-southeast-1.
Role: arn:aws:iam::667836586836:role/hushstore-github-actions-deploy-role

KB-11 chung minh KHONG AI assume duoc role nay (JWT tu ky bi InvalidIdentityToken).
KB-12 tra loi cau con lai: gia su co ai do assume duoc — no lam duoc gi, va khong
lam duoc gi. Dung iam simulate-principal-policy, danh gia tren policy that dang gan.

Lenh dung cho tung dong:

  aws iam simulate-principal-policy \
    --policy-source-arn arn:aws:iam::667836586836:role/hushstore-github-actions-deploy-role \
    --action-names <action> --resource-arns <arn> \
    --query 'EvaluationResults[0].EvalDecision' \
    --profile hushstore --no-cli-pager --output text

Voi ecs:RunTask them:

    --context-entries "ContextKeyName=ecs:cluster,ContextKeyType=string,ContextKeyValues=arn:aws:ecs:ap-southeast-1:667836586836:cluster/hushstore"

LUU Y VE PHUONG PHAP — mot phep simulate thieu context cho ket qua "an toan" GIA:
dong cuoi cung cho thay ecs:RunTask tren dung task definition migrator cung tra ve
implicitDeny khi KHONG truyen context key ecs:cluster. Ly do la statement
EcsRunOnlyMigratorTask co Condition ArnEquals tren ecs:cluster, va simulator coi
mot condition key khong duoc cung cap la khong thoa. Nghia la ai do do "role nay co
chay duoc task khong" ma quen --context-entries se ket luan sai rang no khong chay
duoc gi ca — tuc bo qua chinh duong ma pipeline dung hang ngay.
