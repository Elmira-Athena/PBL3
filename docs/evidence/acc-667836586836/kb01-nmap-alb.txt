### KB-01: quet port ALB tu may tan cong (laptop, 42.1.89.156)

Ghi chu phuong phap: lan dau toi quet toan 65535 port (-p-) va nmap het han
900s ma chua ra ket qua. Nguyen nhan la bang chung, khong phai loi: SG cua ALB

timeout. Neu SG tra 'refused' thi scan se xong trong vai giay. Do do quet lai
theo pham vi khai bao ro: 1000 port pho bien nhat + cac port ung dung/DB/admin.
