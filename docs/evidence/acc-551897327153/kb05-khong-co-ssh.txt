PHAN TICH TRUNG THUC — bang tren co HAI dong proto=-1 from=-1 to=-1.
'-1' nghia la MOI protocol, MOI port, tuc CO BAO GOM 22. Khong duoc bo qua.
Truy ra ca hai:
   sg-0554e1f7ac63016d0 -> default	vpc-0b84a98c407cd4136
   sg-0c963910a01aa5522 -> default	vpc-056b9396279627317

   Ca hai la SECURITY GROUP 'default' — AWS tu tao mot cai cho MOI VPC va
   KHONG cho xoa. Mac dinh cua no la 'cho phep moi traffic tu chinh no'.
   Mot thuoc VPC cua ta (vpc-0b84a98c407cd4136), mot thuoc default VPC.
   Terraform cua ta KHONG quan ly chung va KHONG gan gi vao chung.

   Cau hoi dung phai hoi: co gi dang DUNG chung khong?
$ aws ec2 describe-network-interfaces --filters group-id=<2 SG do>
   so ENI dang dung: 0
   -> 0. Khong resource nao nam trong chung, nen khong co be mat that.

   RUI RO CON LAI (ghi ra thay vi bo qua): neu sau nay co ai launch mot
   instance ma KHONG chi dinh security group, AWS se gan default SG cho no.
   Luc do instance ay nam trong mot SG mo moi port tu chinh no. Cach chan
